Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the ServEase service network.

How are service providers verified?

All technicians must register their full CNIC front/back images, years of experience, and bio profile. Furthermore, they are required to submit a PKR 500 security deposit screenshot, which is manually verified by the administrative team before approval.

Can I request a custom service category?

Yes. If you have a specific trade or service request that is not in the system, providers can submit custom requests directly to our administrators through their dashboards for immediate category addition.

How are booking disputes or complaints resolved?

In case of any issues, either customer or provider can file an in-app complaint linked to the specific booking. Admins review each complaint log and can warn, dismiss, or suspend the accounts of bad actors.

What is the standard commission rate?

We maintain a flat 10% commission rate on total booking values. This commission accumulates in the provider's wallet and is periodically resolved via admin verified payments.
