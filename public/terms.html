2. Service Description & Roles

ServEase serves as a technology platform connecting independent verified home service technicians (Providers) with clients requesting home maintenance, cleaning, tailoring, beauty, education, and crafting services (Customers).

3. Provider Security Deposit Requirement (PKR 500)

Mandatory Security Verification Fee:

All service provider accounts must submit a refundable security deposit of PKR 500 alongside valid CNIC documentation. Accounts remain pending until manual admin approval of the payment receipt.

The PKR 500 security deposit protects platform integrity and ensures accountability during active service bookings.

4. Customer Conduct & Booking Commitments

  • Customers agree to provide accurate location addresses and job descriptions.
  • Rates are billed transparently on hourly or fixed-job agreements confirmed prior to provider arrival.
  • Aggressive behavior, fraud, or non-payment will result in immediate account suspension.

5. Cancellations & Disputes

Either party may cancel a requested job prior to provider dispatch without penalty. For completed jobs, dispute resolution is handled directly by ServEase Administration upon submission of job details and receipts.
